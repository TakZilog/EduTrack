## Design Specificity Verdict

GENERIC. Strip the seal and rewrite three strings and this is a bank portal, a clinic portal, or an HR onboarding page. Palette and seal are product-true; composition, interaction and visual language are not.

Three documented self-contradictions (all verified in source):
- `.identity::before/::after` paint two radial-gradient glow orbs (source comment: "Decorative glowing orbs"), against DESIGN.md's "flat plane of colour... no elevation at all".
- `style.css:485` "Instead of scallops, let's use a sleek overlapping floating card look on desktop" - the documented scalloped edge and its Tangent Rule were deleted for `border-radius:32px` + `margin-left:-20px`.
- `index.html` comment claims "a directory board, not a stack of cards... divided by hairlines"; the CSS beneath builds three cards with gap/border/radius/shadow. Zero hairline dividers exist.

Measured hierarchy failure: lead row is claimed "twice the size" of its peers; measured 130px vs 102px (1.27x) at 1440, 186px vs 123px at 390.

Missed product character: "Admin Building" appears nowhere. No room count (41), no floor count (3), no mention of the gate as fixed BFS origin, no evidence the walkthrough is photographic (~90 real 360 photos exist unused), no echo of the route strip (the system's own named signature component).

Deterministic scan: CLI found 1 finding (overused-font: Inter, index.html:9), exit 2, and self-reported DEGRADED (htmlparser2/css-select/css-tree/domutils unavailable; regex fallback; custom properties, selector matching and computed contrast NOT evaluated - an undercount). Browser injection SUCCEEDED (mutable confirmed), live server on port 8400 started and confirmed stopped; in-page detector found 2 more: low-contrast on `.identity-tagline` and `.locality`, 4.3:1 white on #0072ff.

## What's Working

1. The lead row's description is promoted to body ink and body size while its peers stay muted label size (`.dest-lead .dest-desc { color: var(--text); font-size: var(--fs-body) }`). The one line that defuses the visitor's real fear reads at 17.4:1 / 16px; the account rows recede to 4.83:1 / 14px. Hierarchy without a badge or a "recommended" pill.
2. The seal is handled as an institutional seal, not a logo: 100px, 50% radius, white backing, 4px padding on the blue field. Honours PRODUCT.md's binding commitment literally, and the white ring is what makes it read as a seal. It is what buys trust from a stranger at a gate.
3. Genuinely disciplined state and motion: named transition properties, no `transition: all`, no overshoot easing, hover gated behind `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, `:active` settles the row down rather than up, `:focus-visible` at 7.51:1.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] At 360x640, zero destinations are fully visible; the identity band eats 48% of the viewport
`.identity` renders at a fixed 308px on every phone width (B measured `.identity` 390x308 at 390 wide). At 360x640 the lead row spans y445-656 against a 640 fold; document height 958. destsFullyVisible: 0.
Why: PRODUCT.md principle 3 names low-end phones as day-one usage. The visitor's first act in an enrolment queue is a blind scroll past a logo of a building they are standing inside.
Fix: below 900px collapse `.identity` to the `identity-slim` treatment that already exists at style.css:533-546 (~64px band) - `map/select-room.html` already uses it. Move the campus name into the working surface as one 14px Muted line.
Command: /impeccable adapt

### [P1] The destination rows have effectively no boundary - 1.05:1 fill, 1.08:1 border
`.dest` is #ffffff on `.surface` #f8fafc = 1.05:1; border #edf2f7 = 1.08:1; hover border #cbd5e1 = 1.42:1. DESIGN.md's Two-Greys Rule names #E5E7EB at 1.24:1 as the known previously-shipped failure; this ships LIGHTER than the banned value, on the only interactive controls the page has, missing WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 by a factor of three.
Compounding bug: `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) { .dest:hover { box-shadow: none } }` - because `.dest:hover` outranks `.dest`, hovering DELETES the resting shadow rather than restoring it. For a reduced-motion pointer user the card flattens into the page at the exact moment of intent.
Why: outdoors on a low-end LCD, a 4%-opacity shadow and a 1.08:1 hairline do not exist. Separation and size hierarchy fail together.
Fix: pick one background (`.surface` to #FFFFFF or var(--bg), not a third off-white), give `.dest` `1px solid var(--border-control)` (#6B7280, 4.83:1) as DESIGN.md's Do list mandates, and set the reduced-motion rule to the resting shadow value rather than `none`.
Command: /impeccable polish

### [P1] The primary CTA routes to a content-free interstitial that renames and hedges the promise
`.dest-lead` points at `guest-map.html`: the same 308px band, a back link, "Browse as Guest", the hedge "Some rooms or details may be limited to enrolled students", and one button onward. That is the whole file.
Why: the visitor taps "Find your enrollment room - no account needed" and receives a reframing from task to role plus a fresh doubt, at the moment of commitment, on weak Wi-Fi. Three screens and two page loads separate intent from the room list.
Fix: point `.dest-lead` at `map/select-room.html` directly; put the guest caveat on the lead row or the picker, not on a screen of its own.
Command: /impeccable distill

### [P2] The page never names the building, the gate, the floors, the room count, or the mechanism
The only product-specific string is the tagline, and the lead row's label "Enrollment Room Map" actively reinforces the map-reading model PRODUCT.md says is wrong.
Fix: add `Admin Building - 41 rooms - 3 floors` as a 14px Muted line under "Start here". Rewrite the lead description to state mechanism and origin: "Walk there from the gate in real photos of the building. No account needed."
Command: /impeccable clarify

### [P2] The identity field violates its own flat-plane rule with two glow orbs and a shadowed seal
Two radial-gradient orbs plus `0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.2)` on the seal. The orb-glow hero is the default look of every SaaS template and is the main reason the page reads as generic. The `::after` orb also brightens the region where `.locality` sits, which is where both assessments independently found the contrast risk.
Fix: delete both pseudo-elements and the logo shadow; earn desktop interest from structure instead. Raise `.locality` to 16px/600.
Command: /impeccable quieter

## Qualified / false positive

The browser detector's low-contrast finding on `.identity-tagline` and `.locality` is OVERSTATED, not wrong. It resolved the backdrop to the single lightest gradient stop (#0072FF, 4.33:1) rather than the gradient beneath each text run. Projected along the 135deg axis, most of that text sits in the ~10-13:1 band. But the trailing end of `.locality`'s 435px line projects to ~91% along the axis, landing ~4.3-5.0:1 - and A independently measured ~4.9:1 there. Correct reading: "the gradient endpoint is too light for 14px white body text", not "the tagline fails contrast".

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (Confused First-Timer)**: "Student Login - Log in with your account for full access" is the trap. Jordan is an enrollee, concludes they are a student, reads "full access" as strictly better, and lands in a name/email/password/OTP flow while standing in a queue. A 28px-vs-20px type step does not outrank the word *full*. The recovery path punishes too: the lead row's destination confirms the guest path is "limited". "Enrollment Room Map" suggests the map-reading model they came to avoid. "Admin Building" is not on the page, so there is nothing to match against.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: At 360x640 the entire first screen is facts Casey already knows. The 52px blue disc sits at y=525 on a 640px screen (82% of viewport). "Enrollment Room Map" wraps to two lines at 360 because the disc plus gap take 68px from a 312px row. Peripheral read is "three grey-white blocks" - at 1.05:1 there are no container edges. No theme-color, no favicon link, no meta description; title is bare "EduTrack".

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent)**: `<h1>EduTrack</h1>` and `<h2>Start here</h2>` - no heading names the actual job. The institution is announced twice with two different strings ("ACLC College, Mandaue City" in alt, "ACLC College - Mandaue Campus" in locality). Every landmark is unnamed (header/main/nav, no aria-label). No skip link, with four spoken brand elements ahead of the first link. At 200% zoom the rows have no detectable edge. The reduced-motion rule deletes the only remaining separation on hover.

## Minor Observations

- The radius token layer is dead: `--r-card:12px/--r-btn:10px/--r-input:8px` contradict DESIGN.md and nothing uses them; `.dest` hardcodes 20px, `.btn` 14px.
- Two off-whites differing by 1.01:1 - `#F8F9FA` (token) vs `#f8fafc` (undocumented Tailwind slate-50). The latter is what destroys the card boundary.
- `.surface-lede` measures 4.62:1 - passes AA by 0.12. Moving `.surface` to white restores 4.83:1.
- `assets/img/campus-bg.webp` (119KB) is referenced nowhere - dead weight from the photo-background era.
- Three spellings of the institution across alt text, locality, and the stylesheet header comment.
- Render-blocking third-party font CSS on the named weak-network condition; the 80px wordmark at -0.045em will reflow visibly when Inter lands.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the visitor is standing at the gate with their phone out, why is the first thing you hand them a choice? The gate is the fixed BFS origin and the scope is one building. What does this page look like if its top is the room search field that already exists on `map/select-room.html`?
2. The product's one true piece of wayfinding vocabulary is the route strip - ticks across the top, red for "you are here". Why doesn't the entry surface rhyme with it?
3. Three options are presented as peers, but only one is a thing you *do* here. What breaks if the card stack disappears entirely - one gradient button, and beneath it "Have an account? Log in - Create one"? Lobby boards do not offer you an account.
